# RS
>## The leading eco-AI

## How RS Works
RS is a lightweight AI model built on WR Technology. It leverages simple architecture to run right on your phone; no carbon emmisions, no water usage, no extreme energy costs.
>RS can be used in various use cases, but its not suggested for information sharing
## How to use RS
RS is a small model, and hence cannot preform complex tasks. The WR Technology that powers RS only supports single sentence statements,
but just because of that limitations doesn't mean RS is simply bad.
RS can be used for many cases, certain examples include encouragement and research of WR Technology and similar technologies.
